# Priority Labs / Research Intelligence

This file is a **watch list**, not a prestige list. Labs are ranked by overlap with the target problem.

## Anuruddha Bhattacharjee — UAB / JHU lineage
**Watch for:** magnetic miniature medical robots, magnetic suturing, capsule control, modular millirobots, cardiac flow, low-rate imaging, state estimation, NMPC/autonomy.

**Research overlap:** maximum.

## Eric Diller — University of Toronto Microrobotics Lab
**Technical moat:** miniature magnetic surgical tools, force amplification/transmission, neurosurgical access, pose/sensing integration.

**Questions to mine from their papers**
- How much distal force is practically available?
- How do they package magnets without sacrificing tool function?
- What actuator-to-tool distances are demonstrated?
- What sensing remains wired?

## Pietro Valdastri — University of Leeds / STORM lineage
**Technical moat:** magnetic endoscopy, local magnetic actuation, capsule/endoluminal robots, translational surgical design.

**Mine for:** local magnetic coupling, mechanical transmission, clinical workflow, human studies.

## Sarthak Misra — University of Twente
**Technical moat:** magnetic continuum/catheter systems, actuation, image guidance, capsules, local/hybrid strategies.

**Recent relevant evidence:** 2026 inductively triggered needle-delivery capsule.

## Bradley Nelson / Salvador Pané ecosystem — ETH Zurich
**Technical moat:** clinical magnetic navigation infrastructure, microfabrication, therapeutic microrobots, translation.

**Benchmark:** 2025 Science targeted-therapy platform.

## Metin Sitti / former MPI-IS ecosystem
**Technical moat:** physical intelligence, magnetic locomotion, multimodal soft robots, swarms, biohybrids.

**Mine for:** mechanisms and general principles, not only direct surgery.

## Veronica Iacovacci / Sant’Anna–Leeds ecosystem
**Technical moat:** medical microrobots, fabrication, control, imaging, explicit focus on closing the in-vitro-to-clinic gap.

## David Cappelleri — Purdue
**Technical moat:** tumbling/swimming magnetic microrobots, modular systems, microgrippers/force sensing.

## Aaron Becker — University of Houston
**Technical moat:** global magnetic control, planning, multi-agent/underactuated systems.

## University of Michigan SAM Lab
**Technical moat:** heterogeneous magnetic microrobot swarms and programmable collective behavior.

**Relevance caveat:** strong mechanism/control source; clinical integration still needs separate evidence.
