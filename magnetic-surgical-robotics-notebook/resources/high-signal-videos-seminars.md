## Medical magnetic robotics talks

### Eric Diller — “Micro-Scale Surgery: Using Magnetic Fields to Control Tiny Robots in the Gut and Brain”
TMU Robotics Council / IEEE RAS, 26 May 2026.

https://www.torontomu.ca/engineering-architectural-science/stories-events/events/2026/05/tmu-robotics-council-speaker-series-featuring-eric-diller/

**Why watch**
- direct surgical focus,
- design/production of sub-mm/mm machines,
- externally generated magnetic fields,
- gut + brain use cases,
- PI-level synthesis rather than introductory lecture.

**Notebook task while watching**
Record every shown:
- robot geometry,
- actuation setup,
- force mechanism,
- tracking method,
- clinical phantom/anatomy,
- stated limitation.

## Visual research pages worth inspecting repeatedly

### University of Michigan SAM Lab — microrobot swarm videos
https://samlab.robotics.umich.edu/microrobot-swarms

**Why useful:** actual videos of maze navigation, object transport, rotating collectives, heterogeneous swarm behavior. Good for developing intuition about global-field underactuation and environmental interactions.

### University of Tokyo / Arai lineage — on-chip magnetically driven robotics videos
https://www.biorobotics.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/research/research2_e.html

**Why useful:** historical but concrete magnetic microtool / cell-manipulation mechanisms.
