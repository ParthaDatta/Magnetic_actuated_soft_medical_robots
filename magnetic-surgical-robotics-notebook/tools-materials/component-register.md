# Tools & Materials Register

Only verified components/materials from relevant papers belong here.

## Magnetic materials

### NdFeB
Use cases:
- permanent magnets embedded in rigid tools,
- magnetic tips,
- hard-magnetic soft composites,
- actuation sources.

Record for each experiment:
- grade,
- dimensions,
- remanence / magnetization,
- coating,
- magnetization direction,
- sterilization/biocompatibility relevance.

### Iron-oxide magnetic nanoparticles
Use cases:
- biohybrid/soft microrobots,
- therapeutic payload systems,
- imaging/actuation,
- hyperthermic release.

Key tradeoff:
magnetic responsiveness vs imaging detectability vs cytocompatibility.

## Soft robot materials observed in current literature

### SIL 30
2026 magnetically steerable endonasal suction robot:
- 3D printed biocompatible material,
- 4 mm OD / 2 mm ID device,
- FBG integrated.

### Ecoflex 00-50
Reported in prior soft magnetic suction-device work:
- soft segment,
- permanent magnet embedded,
- >90° in-plane bending under moderate fields (<25 mT reported in source summary).

### PDMS + NdFeB particles
Common magnetic continuum/microrobot composite pattern.
A 2022 intravascular soft continuum microrobot paper reports NdFeB particles in PDMS and ~200 μm-scale diameter capability.

## Shape/force sensing

### Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG)
High-priority for continuum surgical devices because it can provide distributed shape information in a compact form.

Questions to track:
- fiber diameter,
- number/location of gratings,
- interrogator rate,
- calibration procedure,
- temperature compensation,
- sterilization/integration.

---

## Imaging/localization systems to track

- fluoroscopy / X-ray,
- ultrasound,
- OCT,
- endoscopic cameras,
- magnetic localization,
- SMOL-type resonant magnetic tracker,
- hybrid imaging + magnetic sensing.

Do not record only modality name: capture frame rate, accuracy, depth, radiation burden, occlusion behavior, and compatibility with the actuator.
