# Decision — 2026-08-30 — Research notebook policy

## Decision
Use a GitHub-style evidence repository as the primary research memory. Daily reports must update the scientific map, not teach generic subjects.

## Reason
The research goal is long-lived and cumulative. The useful output is traceability:
- what was found,
- where it came from,
- what we tried,
- why a method failed,
- what tools/materials matter,
- what changed in our interpretation,
- which ideas remain plausible after prior-art checks.

## Explicit rejection
Avoid entries such as “learn MATLAB,” “learn COMSOL,” or broad lecture lists unless a specific resource directly supports an active model, experiment, material, control method, or paper reproduction.

## Reversal condition
None anticipated; only repository format may evolve.
